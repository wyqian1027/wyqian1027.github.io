We are always looking for motivated people to join our group. Research in our group spans quantum simulation, quantum algorithms, and high-energy nuclear physics — a genuinely interdisciplinary environment that bridges physics and quantum information science. 我们长期欢迎有志向的同学和研究者加入团队。我们的研究涵盖量子模拟、量子算法与高能核物理，是一个交叉学科的科研环境，连接物理学与量子信息科学。

If you are interested in working with us, please reach out by email with a brief description of your background and research interests. Applications at all levels are welcome. 如有意加入，请发送电子邮件，简要介绍您的学术背景和研究兴趣。我们欢迎各层次的申请。

Postdoctoral Researchers 博士后研究员
Candidates with a strong background in theoretical nuclear/particle physics, quantum information, or related fields are encouraged to apply. Competitive funding is available via CCNU and national postdoctoral programs. Please send your CV, research statement, and the names of 2–3 references to wqian@ccnu.edu.cn. 欢迎具有理论核物理、粒子物理、量子信息或相关领域扎实背景的候选人申请。可通过华师大及国家博士后基金提供有竞争力的资助。请将简历、研究计划及2至3位推荐人信息发送至 wqian@ccnu.edu.cn。
Graduate Students (Ph.D. & M.S.) 研究生（博士 & 硕士）
Prospective graduate students should apply through the official CCNU graduate admissions process. Feel free to contact us beforehand to discuss research directions. Background in quantum mechanics, field theory, or programming (Python/Julia/Qiskit) is a plus. 有意攻读研究生的同学请通过华中师范大学官方招生渠道申请。欢迎提前联系我们交流研究方向。具有量子力学、场论或编程（Python/Julia/Qiskit/ITensor）基础者优先。
Undergraduate Students 本科生
Undergraduate students interested in hands-on research in quantum simulation or computational physics are welcome. We offer summer research projects and opportunities for independent study. No prior research experience is required — curiosity and enthusiasm are what matter. 欢迎对量子模拟或计算物理实践研究感兴趣的本科生。我们提供科研项目和独立研究学习的机会，具有量子力学或编程（Python/Julia）基础者优先。
