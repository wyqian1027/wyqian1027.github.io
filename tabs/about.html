I am currently an Associate Professor at the Institute of Particle Physics, Central China Normal University (CCNU). Previously, I was a Junior Fellow Global Talent and Marie Curie Fellow at the Instituto Galego de Física de Altas Enerxías (IGFAE), University of Santiago de Compostela, Spain. 我现为华中师范大学物理科学与技术学院副研究员。此前，我在西班牙圣地亚哥大学加利西亚高能物理研究所担任全球人才青年学者和玛丽居里博士后研究员。

My research sits at the intersection of high-energy nuclear physics theory and quantum information science. I am particularly focused on quantum simulation of QCD phenomena — jet evolution in matter, finite-temperature non-equilibrium dynamics, and hadronic structure — bridging quantum algorithms with open problems in nuclear and particle physics. 我的研究方向位于高能核物理理论与量子信息科学的交叉领域，专注于QCD现象的量子模拟——包括喷注在物质中的演化、有限温度非平衡动力学以及强子结构，将量子算法与核物理和粒子物理中的前沿问题相结合。
