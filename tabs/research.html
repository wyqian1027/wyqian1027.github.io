Research Overview 研究概述

Our group works at the intersection of high-energy nuclear physics and quantum information science. We develop quantum simulation frameworks, quantum algorithms, and tensor network methods to tackle open problems in QCD, ranging from jet propagation in hot nuclear matter to the non-perturbative structure of hadrons. 我们的研究组工作于高能核物理与量子信息科学的交叉领域，致力于开发量子模拟框架、量子算法以及张量网络方法，以解决QCD中的前沿问题，涵盖喷注在热核物质中的传播以及强子的非微扰结构等。

01

Jets & Medium Effects 喷注与介质效应

We build quantum simulation frameworks for jet evolution inside quark-gluon plasma (QGP). By mapping the parton evolution onto quantum circuits, we can simulate momentum broadening, gluon emission, and entropy growth in real time — calculations that are classically intractable at large system sizes. 我们构建描述喷注在夸克胶子等离子体（QGP）中演化的量子模拟框架。通过将部分子映射到量子线路上，我们能够实时模拟动量展宽、胶子辐射以及熵的增长——这些在大系统尺寸下是经典计算难以处理的问题。

02

Hadron Structure & Parton Distributions 强子结构与部分子分布

Using both quantum computers and tensor networks, we compute parton distribution functions (PDFs), distribution amplitudes, and form factors of mesons and baryons. Tensor network methods (MPS/MPO) allow us to access regions unreachable by exact diagonalization. 我们利用量子计算机和张量网络，计算介子与重子的部分子分布函数（PDF）、分布振幅及形状因子。张量网络方法（MPS/MPO）使我们能够访问精确对角化无法触及的物理问题。

03

Real-time & Thermal Dynamics 实时与热场动力学

We design quantum algorithms for simulating finite-temperature non-equilibrium dynamics, including heavy quark thermalization and initial stage equilibration effect. These methods address open questions about how QCD matter reaches thermal equilibrium after a heavy-ion collision. 我们设计量子算法以模拟有限温度非平衡动力学，包括重夸克热化和初始态平衡效应。这些方法有助于解答重离子碰撞后QCD物质如何达到热平衡的前沿问题。

04

Quantum Algorithms & Computing 量子算法与量子计算

Beyond physics-specific applications, we develop and benchmark general quantum algorithms — variational methods (VQE, QAOA), quantum signal processing (QSP) and circuit cutting strategies — with an eye toward near-term hardware, fault-tolerant algorithm, analog quantum simulators, and also quantum machine learning. 除物理专项应用外，我们还开发和测试通用量子算法——变分方法（VQE、QAOA）、量子信号处理（QSP）以及线路切割策略——着眼于近期硬件、容错算法、模拟量子模拟器以及量子机器学习。
